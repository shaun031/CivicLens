# Department operations and interpretation

`/admin/analytics/departments` compares current selected incident cohorts by assigned department.
Management remains in existing Phase9 screens. Department metrics include active incidents, current
officer-assigned incidents, awaiting-officer incidents, in-progress work, current critical/overdue
incidents, weighted active workload, eligible available staff and valid recorded resolution counts.
Unallocated active work is separate; it does not silently disappear into a fictional department.

Response summaries use the earliest valid assignment in the current open cycle. Statistics include
sample size/mean/median/min/max. Response compliance is MET/(MET+MISSED); pending/unavailable outcomes
are separate. Current policies are hypothetical operational targets, not legal service guarantees.
Recorded100% progress milestones are distinctly labelled and are not verified completed repairs.
Recorded-operation trends count actual event timestamps for the current department/cohort, not
historical staffing/responsibility reconstructed from today's assignment. Historical ownership can
change after reassignment, merge or split; do not interpret this as an immutable longitudinal ranking.

Comparison checkboxes, workload/priority charts, response context, tables and authorized incident
drill-down retain relevant filters. Severity, responsibilities, incident complexity, geography,
resources, staffing and missing records differ. No “best/worst department” or productivity ranking
is calculated. High workload can reflect greater demand or understaffing, not poor performance.
